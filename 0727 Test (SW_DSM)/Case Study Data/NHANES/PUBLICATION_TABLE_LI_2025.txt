FOOTNOTES:

All analyses use 200 bootstrap replications for confidence intervals.
All p-values are two-sided.
Sample: Study sample ATE (unweighted)
Population: Population ATE (PATE, survey-weighted with WTMEC2YR)

INTERPRETATION:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Higher dietary quality (fiber intake) reduces hypertension odds by:
  • 11-15% in study sample (unweighted methods)
  • 17-34% in US population (survey-weighted methods)
  
Ignoring survey weights underestimates the protective effect by 48%,
leading to incorrect public health recommendations.

All methods show statistically significant protective effects 
(all 95% CIs exclude OR = 1.0).
