## 📊 **TOP 10 RESEARCH QUESTIONS**

### **TIER 1: READY TO ANALYZE** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

#### **1. Gender Differences in Alcohol Use Frequency**
- **Outcome**: Days of alcohol use (past month/year)
- **Treatment**: Female vs. Male
- **Sample**: All adults (N ≈ 40,000+)
- **Why Priority**: Well-studied prevalence, understudied frequency, clinical relevance

#### **2. Gender Differences in Marijuana Use Frequency**  
- **Outcome**: Days of marijuana use (past 30)
- **Treatment**: Female vs. Male
- **Sample**: Past-month marijuana users (N ≈ 5,000-8,000)
- **Why Priority**: Legalization trends, policy relevance, gender patterns

#### **3. Employment Status → Binge Drinking Frequency**
- **Outcome**: Days of binge drinking (past month)
- **Treatment**: Employed vs. Unemployed
- **Sample**: Working-age adults (N ≈ 30,000+)
- **Why Priority**: Economic stress pathway, workplace interventions

---

### **TIER 2: GOOD QUESTIONS** ⭐⭐⭐⭐

#### **4. Insurance Status → Substance Use Frequency**
- **Outcome**: Days of various substance use
- **Treatment**: Insured vs. Uninsured
- **Why**: Healthcare access, treatment pathways, policy

#### **5. Education → Drinking Intensity**
- **Outcome**: Average drinks per occasion
- **Treatment**: College degree vs. no college
- **Why**: Protective factor, intervention targeting

#### **6. Age Group → Substance Use Frequency**
- **Outcome**: Days of substance use
- **Treatment**: Young adult (18-25) vs. Adult (26+)
- **Why**: Developmental transitions, prevention timing

---

### **TIER 3: EXPLORATORY** ⭐⭐⭐

#### **7. Mental Health Treatment → Substance Use**
- **Outcome**: Days of substance use
- **Treatment**: MH treatment (yes/no)
- **Challenge**: Reverse causation concerns

#### **8. Race/Ethnicity → Use Patterns**
- **Outcome**: Days of substance use
- **Treatment**: Various racial comparisons
- **Why**: Health equity, structural determinants

#### **9. Marital Status → Alcohol Frequency**
- **Outcome**: Days of drinking
- **Treatment**: Married vs. unmarried
- **Why**: Social support mechanisms

#### **10. SUD Severity → Treatment Intensity**
- **Outcome**: Treatment days/episodes
- **Treatment**: Mild vs. severe SUD
- **Why**: Treatment matching, resource allocation

## ⚠️ **IMPORTANT CAVEATS**

### **1. Variable Coding Issues**:
- Days variables use special missing codes (91-99)
- Need proper recoding before analysis
- Check codebook for exact definitions

### **2. Zero-Inflation**:
- Many people have 0 days of use
- May need to restrict to users
- OR model use (binary) + frequency given use (continuous)

### **3. Causality**:
- Cross-sectional NSDUH data
- Need strong justification for causal direction
- Sensitivity analyses critical
- Discuss limitations openly

### **4. Publication Requirements**:
- Need clear motivating example
- Strong literature foundation
- Novel contribution articulated
- Clinical/policy implications
