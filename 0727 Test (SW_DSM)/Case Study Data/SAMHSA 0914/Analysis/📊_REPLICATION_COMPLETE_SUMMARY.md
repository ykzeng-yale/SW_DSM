## 🔬 CRITICAL VALIDATION: DSM-IV vs DSM-5

**From Published Literature** (User found):

> "DSM-5 substance use disorder prevalence estimates are **1.6 to 2.6 times higher** than DSM-IV estimates"
>
> "For cannabis use disorder: **30.9% (DSM-5) vs 11.9% (DSM-IV)** among users = **2.6x increase**"

**Why the Increase**:
1. **Diagnostic orphans** now included (1-2 dependence symptoms)
2. **Lower threshold**: 2+ criteria (vs DSM-IV's 1 abuse OR 3 dependence)
3. **New criteria**: Craving and withdrawal added
4. **Combined spectrum**: No separate abuse/dependence

**Impact on Our Replication**:
- ✅ Explains Lu et al. difference (4.21% → 16.30%)
