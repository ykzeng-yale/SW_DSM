GROUP 1: NSDUH 2019-2023 (MOST RECENT)
================================================================

1. Ali et al. (2022) - NSDUH 2019
   - Citation: Ali, A., et al. (2022). Association of Cannabis Use Disorder and Other Psychiatric Disorders with Treatment Seeking. Journal of Addiction Medicine, 16(3):329-335
   - PMC: PMC8963574
   - NSDUH Years: 2019 ONLY
   - Sample: N=3,425 adults 18+ with cannabis use disorder
   - Research Question: Does comorbid psychiatric disorder (MDE/anxiety) predict treatment seeking among adults with CUD?
   - Treatment/Exposure: Having MDE or anxiety disorder (among those with CUD)
   - Outcome: Past-year substance use treatment seeking (binary)
   - Method: Survey-weighted logistic regression
   - Key Results: MDE aOR = 1.45 (1.07-1.97) for treatment seeking
   - Status: ✅ Can replicate - have NSDUH 2023 data

2. Mojtabai (2022) - NSDUH 2018-2019
   - Citation: Mojtabai, R. (2022). Estimating Prevalence of SUDs using Benchmark Multiplier Method. JAMA Psychiatry, 79(11):1074-1080
   - PMC: PMC9494265
   - NSDUH Years: 2018 AND 2019 (pooled)
   - Sample: NSDUH participants with SUDs (N varies by disorder)
   - Research Question: What is true SUD prevalence (combining survey + Medicaid data)?
   - Treatment/Exposure: N/A (prevalence estimation study, not causal)
   - Method: Benchmark multiplier method (NOT regression/matching)
   - Key Results: Cannabis UD 7.57% (vs NSDUH 1.68%), Alcohol UD 20.27% (vs NSDUH 5.34%)
   - Status: ❌ NOT a causal study - prevalence estimation only

GROUP 2: NSDUH 2011-2019 (RECENT, AVAILABLE TO US)
================================================================

3. Lu et al. (2023) - NSDUH 2011-2019
   - Citation: Lu, W., et al. (2023). Substance use disorder and treatment among young adults aged 18-25. Drug Alcohol Depend Rep, 8:100181
   - PMC: PMC10430156
   - NSDUH Years: 2011-2019 (pooled 9 years)
   - Sample: N=207,895 young adults 18-25 (pooled), ~23,099 per year
   - Research Question: What are sociodemographic predictors of SUD prevalence and treatment use?
   - Treatment/Exposure: Sociodemographic characteristics (not causal intervention)
   - Outcomes: Any SUD prevalence, treatment receipt among those with SUD
   - Method: Survey-weighted multivariable logistic regression
   - Key Results: Female vs Male aOR=0.53 (0.50-0.55) for SUD; Age 22-25 vs 18-21 aOR=0.66 (0.63-0.69)
   - Cannabis UD 2019: 4.21% (DSM-IV)
   - Status: ✅ SUCCESSFULLY REPLICATED with NSDUH 2023 (demographics matched, SUD rates differ due to DSM-5)

GROUP 3: NSDUH 2008-2013 (OLDER BUT REPLICABLE)
================================================================

4. Pacek et al. (2017) - NSDUH 2008-2013
   - Citation: Pacek, L.R., et al. (2017). Age of first cannabis use and Axis I psychiatric disorders. J Clin Psychiatry, 78(1):e63-e70
   - PMC: PMC5411709
   - NSDUH Years: 2008-2013 (pooled 6 years)
   - Sample: N=53,737 adolescents/young adults (12-25) with lifetime cannabis use
   - Research Question: Does earlier age of first cannabis use CAUSE higher rates of psychiatric disorders?
   - Treatment/Exposure: Age of first cannabis use (categorical: ≤12, 13-14, 15-16, 17-18, vs 19+ reference)
   - Outcomes: Past-year MDE, suicidal ideation, suicide attempt
   - Method: Survey-weighted logistic regression, controlling for confounders
   - Key Results: 
     * Age ≤12 → MDE: aOR = 1.41 (1.09-1.82)
     * Age ≤12 → Suicidal ideation: aOR = 1.86 (1.40-2.48)
     * Age ≤12 → Suicide attempt: aOR = 2.50 (1.69-3.70)
     * Gradient effect: Earlier use → worse outcomes
   - Status: ✅ SUCCESSFULLY REPLICATED with NSDUH 2023 (N=8,702, 97% of their per-year average, patterns matched)

GROUP 5: NSDUH 2005-2007 (OLDER)
================================================================

6. Hingson et al. (2009) - NSDUH 2005-2007
   - Citation: Hingson, R.W., et al. (2009). Early age of first drunkenness. Drug Alcohol Review, 28(1):2-14
   - NSDUH Years: 2005-2007 (pooled 3 years)
   - Sample: Large adult samples (N varies by analysis)
   - Research Question: Does early age of first drunkenness CAUSE mental health problems?
   - Treatment/Exposure: Age of first drunkenness (≤14, 15-17, 18-20, vs 21+ reference)
   - Outcomes: Past-year MDE, alcohol dependence
   - Method: Survey-weighted logistic regression
   - Key Results:
     * First drunk ≤14 → MDE: aOR = 1.5 (1.3-1.8)
     * First drunk ≤14 → Alcohol dependence: aOR = 4.9 (4.1-5.8)
   - Status: ✅ Can replicate with NSDUH 2023 (methods clear)
