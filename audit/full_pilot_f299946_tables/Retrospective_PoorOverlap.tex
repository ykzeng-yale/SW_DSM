WDSM additionally uses survey weights in matched imputation, bias correction, and H\'ajek normalization. SW-PSM retains an always-correct treatment-interacted outcome regression. Signed relative bias is $100(\overline{\widehat\tau}-\tau)/|\tau|$. Relative efficiency is the Monte Carlo variance of PSM under Cor|Cor divided by that of each method within the same estimand, sampling design, and overlap. PSM, PGM, and DSM use $M=1$ matching with replacement; SW-PSM uses optimal full matching; WDSM uses $M\in\{1,3,5\}$. All intervals are centered normal Wald intervals. WDSM uses fixed-original-reuse replication variance with divisor $B$; PSM, PGM, and DSM retain the author's bootstrap sample variance with divisor $B-1$; SW-PSM uses subclass-cluster sandwich/delta-method variance. Bootstrap methods use $B=200$.
